Our Story

Founded in 2010, Maison Élégance began as a boutique interior design studio with a singular vision: to create spaces that transcend trends and embody timeless sophistication.

Over the years, we have cultivated relationships with master craftsmen and artisans worldwide, allowing us to source and create pieces that are as unique as our clients. Our design philosophy marries classical proportions with contemporary sensibilities, resulting in interiors that feel both grand and intimately livable.
